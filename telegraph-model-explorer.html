Telegraph Model Explorer

Two-state promoter: OFF → ON at kon, ON → OFF at koff, initiation rate s when ON, degradation d = 1. All rates in units of d.

Parameters

Exact moments

Promoter ON fraction P ∼ Beta(kon, koff)

Stationary law is Beta-Poisson: N | P ∼ Poisson(s·P) with P Beta-distributed.

How to read this

kon controls how often bursts start: smaller kon pushes mass to k = 0 (dropout) and lengthens the tail.

koff sets burst duration: larger koff gives shorter, sharper bursts, smaller Fano.

s scales production per unit ON time: raising s moves the mean up and amplifies overdispersion (Fano → 1 + s·koff/((kon+koff)(kon+koff+1))).

The orange curve is the exact stationary pmf from the chemical master equation (adaptive support, K = ); the blue bars are 2000 Gillespie-simulated cells.

References: Peccoud & Ycart, Bull. Math. Biol. 57:795 (1995); Shahrezaei & Swain, PNAS 105:17256 (2008).

12-cell cohort (scRNA-seq style snapshot at a shared time)

The dish holds the same 12 cells at the sampling time t*, fixed at 80% of the window. Fill opacity scales with mRNA count (50 transcripts = fully opaque for now); zero-count cells stay empty (dish background). This mimics an scRNA-seq experiment: each cell is lysed and counted once, at one shared moment, with no access to its history. The histogram in the middle summarizes the 12 snapshot counts; the mock tissue on the right shows the same heterogeneity in situ: adjacent cells sharing a tissue can still differ in expression purely from stochastic promoter switching.
